# Edit a ClipHunter clip

1. Open a project and choose **Edit clip** on a suggested highlight, or choose **Edit clip** in **Clips**. A recorded stream initially downloads only a short source window around each audience reaction.
2. Choose **Vertical focus** for the tall right-side preview, **Classic** for a central preview, or **Compact** for a smaller screen. Drag the left edge of the right preview column to resize it. The layout and width are remembered. Toggle **Safe zones** on the preview to see the bottom and right areas where short-form platforms often place controls.
3. Drag **IN** and **OUT** on the timeline. The source frame and vertical composition follow each handle. Click elsewhere on the ruler to move the playhead. You can type `mm:ss` or `hh:mm:ss` into the time fields. Keep the selected duration between 20 seconds and 3 minutes. **Play selection** plays only that interval. The audio waveform and chat reaction marker help locate the moment. Add a marker with **M**, toggle trim snapping with **N**, and use **Undo/Redo** or ⌘Z/⌘⇧Z for trim, crop, layout, and caption changes.
4. When you reach the first or last second of the downloaded source, the editor fetches another minute automatically. **Download 1 min earlier/later** does the same on demand. A progress bar shows the measured transfer percentage and ETA where available. Wait for the source to finish joining before changing the trim again.
5. Use the **Frame** tab in the inspector. Gameplay + facecam is the default. Choose **Gameplay** or **Facecam**, then drag the matching box over the source video. Drag its lower right corner to resize it. Set the facecam height, and place it above or below gameplay. The vertical composition updates as you work.
6. Use the **Captions** tab to turn captions on only when wanted. Choose **Auto detect** or a spoken language. Select captions in the original speech language or a supported translation language, then press **Generate captions**. The local Whisper large-v3 model processes only the selected clip; the first use downloads several gigabytes to your chosen model folder. Correct any words and timestamps, especially names, slang, and mixed language speech. Choose a visual caption template, font, colors and size, or leave **No captions** selected. **Download SRT** exports the saved lines as a separate subtitle file. The model does not generate captions during highlight scanning or clip creation.
7. Open **Text** and press **Add text at playhead** to place a title or callout. Set its IN/OUT times, wording, size, colors and position. Drag the text directly on the vertical preview, or use the position sliders. Click its block on the **TXT** timeline track to jump to it. The same text is included in the preview and final export.
8. Changes save after you pause for about a second. The 480p MP4 preview then renders automatically; a progress bar shows measured progress and ETA. For the final file, choose **Export 720×1280** or **Export 1080×1920**. The app downloads only the selected high quality range before rendering. Download the finished MP4 from the editor or **Clips**.

If chat replay is missing or produces no clear peaks, the scanner checks the hour's audio without downloading its video. Audio-based suggestions are estimates, so review them before export. If neither source works, create a manual clip at a specific stream time or choose **Rescan this hour**. If YouTube requests a bot check, complete it yourself and follow the [YouTube access guide](YOUTUBE_ACCESS_GUIDE.md). The app cannot bypass that check. A cut request may still transfer extra bytes depending on the video format and server; the app does not intentionally save the whole VOD for recorded streams.

The **Storage** menu in a project reports downloaded source size. **Delete downloaded hour video** removes the saved source windows and older hour source, while keeping finished MP4s and previews. Editing or exporting those clips then needs the source to be downloaded again.
